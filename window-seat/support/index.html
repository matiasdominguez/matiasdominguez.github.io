Window Seat: Rain Race

Support

Race raindrops down a rainy window.

How to play

  • Drag anywhere on the screen to steer your drop.
  • Swallow smaller drops to grow. Bigger drops fall faster.
  • Dodge bigger drops: they swallow you.
  • Moving drops shrink, so keep eating or you dry up.
  • The first drop to reach the bottom of the window wins.

Windows

  • Parked car: drops run straight down.
  • Taxi: wipers squeeze drops smaller. Their side of the glass is fast; the far side is safe.
  • Night train: wind blows small drops sideways. Steer against it.

Finish a trip of five races to open the next window. Coins open new cities.

Common questions

I hear no sound.

Turn off Silent mode on your iPhone, and check that the title screen says "Sound on".

Will I lose my progress?

Your progress lives on your phone. Deleting the game erases it.

What does the tracking question do?

It decides whether the game may share your advertising identifier with Tenjin, which measures which ads bring players to the game. The game plays the same either way. The privacy policy has the details.
