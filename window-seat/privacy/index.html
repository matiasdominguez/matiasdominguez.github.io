Window Seat: Rain Race

Privacy policy

Effective October 6, 2026

Window Seat: Rain Race is a game by Matias Dominguez, an independent developer. This page explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What stays on your phone

Your progress (coins, windows, cities and best times) and your settings stay on your iPhone and never leave it. Deleting the game deletes them.

What the game sends, and to whom

The game uses Tenjin, a service that measures mobile ad campaigns. When you open the game, and when you finish a race or a trip, the game sends Tenjin:

  • the identifier Apple gives each app on your phone (IDFV), and Apple's advertising identifier (IDFA) only if you allow tracking;
  • your phone model, iOS version, language and time zone;
  • your IP address, from which Tenjin estimates your country;
  • what you did in the game: when you opened it, how long you played, and which races and trips you finished, with your place;
  • Apple's ad attribution data, which tells whether an ad led to your install.

Why

To learn which ads bring players to the game and how many keep playing. The game shows no ads and sells nothing. I do not sell your data, and I do not use it to build a profile of you.

Your choice about tracking

Before iOS asks whether the game may track you, the game explains why. If you allow tracking, Tenjin receives your advertising identifier. You can change your answer at any time in Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Tracking. The game plays the same either way.

What the game never collects

No account, name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.

Tenjin

Tenjin processes this data on my behalf under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly collect their data.

Deleting your data

To have data tied to your phone deleted, email me. I will ask Tenjin to delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, this page will show the new text and a new date.
